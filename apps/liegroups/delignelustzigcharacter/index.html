Deligne–Lusztig Character

Given a connected reductive group G\mathbf{G}G and an FFF-stable maximal torus Tw\mathbf{T}_wTw​ (parameterised by an element www of the Weyl group WWW), the Deligne–Lusztig character RTw(1)R_{\mathbf{T}_w}(1)RTw​​(1) is the virtual character of G(Fq)\mathbf{G}(\mathbb{F}_q)G(Fq​) obtained by ℓ\ellℓ-adic cohomology of the Deligne–Lusztig variety associated to www. This tool expands RTw(1)R_{\mathbf{T}_w}(1)RTw​​(1) in the basis of unipotent characters, displaying the integer inner products ⟨γ,RTw(1)⟩\langle \gamma, R_{\mathbf{T}_w}(1)\rangle⟨γ,RTw​​(1)⟩ for each unipotent character γ\gammaγ. The torus Tw\mathbf{T}_wTw​ can be specified either by choosing a ϕ\phiϕ-conjugacy class of WWW from a drop-down list, or by typing a word in the Coxeter generators (with 1-based indices, separated by spaces).
