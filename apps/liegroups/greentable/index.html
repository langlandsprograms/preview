Green Table

Displays the Green functions QwFQ_{wF}QwF​, corresponding to GreenTable(uc, W) in GAP3/Chevie. These are defined by QwF=∑u,ϕϕ~(wF) X~u,ϕQ_{wF} = \sum_{u,\phi} \tilde{\phi}(wF)\, \tilde{X}_{u,\phi}QwF​=∑u,ϕ​ϕ~​(wF)X~u,ϕ​, where the sum ranges over all local systems (u,ϕ)(u, \phi)(u,ϕ) and X~u,ϕ\tilde{X}_{u,\phi}X~u,ϕ​ are the (normalised) characteristic functions of the intersection cohomology complexes. The rows are indexed by conjugacy classes of the relative Weyl groups WG(L)W_\mathbf{G}(\mathbf{L})WG​(L) attached to the cuspidal local systems; the columns are the local systems LjL_jLj​ (default) or unipotent classes uju_juj​. For the principal Springer series, where L=T\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{T}L=T is a maximal torus, the function QwFQ_{wF}QwF​ coincides with the restriction to the unipotent elements of the Deligne–Lusztig character RTwG(1)R^{\mathbf{G}}_{\mathbf{T}_w}(1)RTw​G​(1). A Y-table toggle shows the companion matrix used in the Lusztig–Shoji algorithm.
